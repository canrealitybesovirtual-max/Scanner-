HOUSE SCANNER

WebXR · Quest 3 / 3S

Walk your whole house in passthrough and it builds a 2 cm 3D point map of every surface from the headset's depth sensor. It adds the room layout from Space Setup (walls, floor, furniture and room mesh) and saves it all so you can continue later.

  • Walk slowly. Look at every surface from 0.5–3 m away, including floors, ceilings, under tables and behind doors.
  • Surfaces take on their real colours from the headset camera as you scan. New detail flashes briefly; amber means look again from another angle.
  • Left hand holds the control panel. Point and pull the right trigger to press a button.
  • Saves stay in this browser on the headset. Export a file to keep a copy.
